# AMPAY Detection

This document defines the public criteria for identifying AMPAY records: documented contradictions between electoral promises and later congressional voting positions.

## Definition

An AMPAY is published when a concrete electoral promise is in documented tension with a later congressional position from the same party.

Required conditions:

- The promise comes from a reviewable government plan or electoral document.
- The vote is directly related to the promise.
- The party position is backed by congressional voting records.
- The record includes stable vote references.

## Required Evidence

Each confirmed AMPAY should include:

- Promise text and source reference.
- Related vote identifier or stable vote reference.
- Expected position implied by the promise.
- Observed caucus position in the vote.
- Scope note when evidence is limited.

## Review Flow

1. Select concrete, verifiable promises.
2. Map related votes by topic and legislative context.
3. Compare the promise, expected vote, and observed vote.
4. Review candidate cases manually before publication.
5. Publish only records with sufficient traceability.

## Constraints

- The vote dataset ends on 2024-07-26.
- No published AMPAY does not mean no contradiction exists outside the analyzed scope.
- Political motives not documented in the vote are not inferred.
- Ambiguous cases stay out of confirmed AMPAY records.
